Questions?

Who is this for?
People with strong ideas who struggle to make them land: professionals, students and job seekers. If you over-explain, stay quiet in meetings or soften recommendations, it’s built for you.
How much time does it take?
25–40 minutes a day for 30 days. If a real conversation isn’t available, run a simulation and label it honestly.
What do I need to use it?
A browser for the tracker, a PDF reader, and Excel or Google Sheets for the premium tracker. A phone recorder helps for the baseline.
Will it get me promoted?
No system can promise that. It trains behaviours you control and helps you collect evidence of change.
